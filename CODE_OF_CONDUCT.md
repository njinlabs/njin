# Code of Conduct

We want njin to be a welcoming place for everyone, whatever their experience, background or identity.

## Our standards

Encouraged:

- Being kind and patient, especially with newcomers
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the project and the community

Not acceptable:

- Harassment, insults, personal attacks or discriminatory language
- Trolling, or publishing someone's private information
- Anything else that would be inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and other contributions that do not follow this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone who repeatedly violates it.

Report problems privately to **halo@jadiweb.id**. Reports are handled in confidence.

This code is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
